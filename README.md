## Abstract
This paper provides a comprehensive survey of the integration of Artificial Intelligence in the educational sector. It specifically explores the advancements and implementation of:
1.  **Automated Assessment Systems:** Streamlining grading and feedback.
2.  **Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS):** Providing personalized instruction without human intervention.
3.  **Adaptive Learning Systems:** Tailoring educational content to meet individual student needs in real-time.
